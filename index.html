Welcome

Two kilometres off the highway, and a different pace entirely.

Turn off the Nairobi–Nyeri road and the noise drops away almost immediately — TD Gardens sits on grounds kept green and open, built for the kind of afternoon that isn't in a hurry to end.

The kitchen runs Kenyan classics — kienyeji chicken, nyama choma by the kilo, tilapia, mukimo — cooked to order, not held under a lamp. The bar carries everything from a Tusker to a Nederburg to a proper tot list, so the table can order however it wants to spend the evening.
